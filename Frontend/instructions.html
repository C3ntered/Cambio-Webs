How to Play Cambio

Cambio is a memory game: finish with the lowest score. Most of your cards stay face down the whole game, so every peek, swap, and guess counts.

Quick Start

Goal End with the lowest total card score.
Memory At the start, memorize your bottom two cards.
Turns Draw, swap or discard, then use any card ability.
  1. Each player starts with face-down cards in a two-row grid and briefly memorizes the bottom row.
  2. On your turn, draw from the deck or take the top of the discard pile.
  3. Drew from the deck? Swap it into your hand, or discard it and use its ability if it has one.
  4. Drew from the discard pile? You must swap it into your hand - no ability, no discarding it back.
  5. Think a face-down card matches the top discard's rank? Eliminate it - on anyone's turn, including your own.
  6. When you think your hand is the lowest, call Cambio at the start of your turn to trigger the final round.

FAQ

How do eliminations work?

If you think a face-down card matches the top discard's rank, click it - on any turn, even someone else's. Guess right and that card is eliminated. Eliminate an opponent's card and you must give them one of your own cards as a replacement.

What happens if I guess wrong?

You draw a penalty card and, if it was your turn, your turn ends.

When should I call Cambio?

Call Cambio at the start of your turn, before drawing, when you think your total is lower than everyone else's. Every other player then gets one last turn before scores are counted, and your own hand is locked in and can no longer be swapped or eliminated.

Simple Turn Flow

  1. Draw a card.
  2. Swap or discard.
  3. Resolve any ability.
  4. Watch for elimination chances.

Lowest score wins, then fewer cards, then the Cambio caller. If still tied, tied players reveal draws until one card has the lowest value.
